Frequently Asked Questions

Which examinations does ByteAcademy cover?
ByteAcademy is designed specifically for GATE CSE and Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) Computer Science examinations, including Polytechnic Assistant Lecturer, PGT (+2 Computer Teacher), and TGT (Secondary Computer Teacher).
Can I study subject by subject?
Yes. The platform organises all 10 core Computer Science subjects with dedicated notes, topic weightage, question banks, and subject-wise practice tests so you can learn systematically.
Are mock tests and previous-year questions included?
Yes. ByteAcademy includes full-length mock tests, subject-wise tests, chapter-wise drills, and solved previous-year question archives for both GATE CSE and BPSC CSE exams.
Can I track my preparation and weak areas?
Progress tracking and performance analytics are built into the student account experience, allowing you to monitor accuracy, completion rates, and test scores over time.
